HOW TO PLAY

🏈 GOAL: Carry the ball into the yellow end zone on the right.
⌨️ MOVE: WASD or arrow keys. On touch screens use the direction buttons.
🎯 PASS: Press J or ENTER to throw to the nearest open receiver. One pass per play.
⚡ DASH: SPACE for a burst of speed and brief tackle protection. Has a cooldown.
💥 TACKLE: A defender touching the ball carrier ends the down.
🏆 SCORE: Touchdown = 7 points and coins. Earn more with multiple touchdowns.
🔧 MOD MENU: Toggle infinite coins, turbo, invincibility, and extra time.
🔄 RESET: A new game resets the match timer, but not your upgrades.
